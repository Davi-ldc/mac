Campaign of 2026-11-21, MacBook Pro Mac17,10 (M5 Max), macOS 27.2.1, on battery,
display at 1497x977 pt, 120 Hz, brightness 42%, a maximized opaque terminal in front.
The wallpaper was visible only behind the Dock (60 pt strip) and the menu bar (39 pt).

The livewall scenarios were measured with an earlier Rust build of the same design
(same window, same video layer, AVAssetReader instead of a sample cursor).

What to keep in mind when reading these numbers:

- Blocks were 8 s after a 2 s settle. System power lags a state change by several
  seconds, so the paired differences are biased low by roughly 8%. The harness now
  defaults to 10 s after 6 s.

- From about 18:07 something else was animating on screen (paused WindowServer near
  33% instead of 19%), most likely the terminal UI of the agent that launched the
  campaign. A second animator already pays part of the cost of running the display
  pipeline, so every difference measured after that is a lower bound: full60, full30,
  edge120 and full120-4k. The floors after the first one and the brightness sweep are
  inflated for the same reason and are not comparable with each other.

- phosphene: after SIGCONT it took about 10 s to return to full rate, so its paired
  mean (3.75 W) understates it. Blocks measured 12 to 20 s after resume average 8.69 W
  against 4.65 W paused.

- The display-mode scenarios (native resolution, panel fixed at 60 Hz) were removed:
  the mode switch did not outlive the tool that made it, so they had run in the home
  mode. The tool is fixed; the scenarios were not run again.

Solid: floor (3.9 W), full120 (+3.2 W paired, +3.5 W steady), dot120, dock120, and the
display rail reading 2.88 to 2.89 W for any 120 Hz update against 1.98 W static.
